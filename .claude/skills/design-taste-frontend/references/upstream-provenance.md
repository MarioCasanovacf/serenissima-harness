The adaptation preserves the upstream brief inference, three dials, anti-default discipline,
design-system honesty, redesign audit, motion and accessibility guardrails, anti-pattern review,
and preflight intent. It reorganizes those ideas into a stack-neutral router and one-level
references for progressive disclosure. It does not vendor the upstream 1,206-line skill
byte-for-byte and does not inherit upstream's default React, Next.js, Tailwind, Motion, imagegen,
or web assumptions.

The complete upstream MIT license is retained in ../LICENSE.upstream. Keep the URL, commit, license
file, and this note together when redistributing or updating the adaptation.
