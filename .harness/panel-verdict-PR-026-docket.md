**Convened:** 2026-08-15, by operator ruling D9 — *"yo creo que hay que abrirlo... ¿Cómo
evitamos que tengamos cebadores de arquitectura? Necesito que el panel me traiga
argumentos."* Arguments were asked for, on both sides. Not a verdict.

**Composition:** four lenses, none aware of the others, convened BEFORE any instrument was
drafted rather than after — the inverse of the PR-022 sequence. Constitutional path;
mechanism design; attack; comparative institutions and the case for not opening.

**Question:** can a typed citation be made to cost the CITER something, without producing
the harms PR-026 refuses?

---

Nobody voted to lift clause 3. Nobody voted to refuse the docket. Including the panelist

## 1. The premise of the docket is inverted, and that is the finding

The attack lens established, by reproduction, that citation in this registry is not
costless. **It is costless upward and free downward.**

| Attack | Cost to attacker | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Overrule any tier-1 record | 1 command, **68 ms** | target's author moves `active 9, overruled 0` -> `active 8, overruled 1` |
| Mint `verification_verdicts_upheld` 0 -> 51 | **9.1 s**, two invented strings | the figure PR-025 calls scarcest, counterfeited |
| Zero anyone's consolidation count | 1 command, **62 ms** | one `distinguishes` sets C=0; the destroyed count belonged to two other identities |
| Zero a verifier's figure via `events.jsonl` | 34 appended lines, **31 ms** | 34 -> 0, untracked file, `git status` stays clean |
| Deny the whole standing surface | **9 bytes** | one unparseable line, `reputation.py standing` exits 4 for every identity |
| Own 77% of the registry | 200 records, **1.42 s** | plus 167 spurious reconsideration candidates, drowning the decay signal |

So PR-022 clause 4 — "NO CONSEQUENCE ATTACHES. NONE" — **is already false in the
destructive direction.** Identities are punished by another agent's free action, and the
number merely reports it. Refusing the docket does not preserve clause 4. It preserves the
belief that clause 4 holds.

**And the safety feature is a targeting readout.** `overrules` refuses without
`--acknowledge-reliance` and prints the citer list first. Under a citer-liability regime
that refusal becomes an aiming reticle: pick a record with many citers, read the casualty
list off the refusal, re-run with one flag, every citer takes the hit simultaneously. The
attacker pays 68 ms and is not identifiable. That is the strongest single argument against
the instrument the docket was opened to consider.

## 6. What every comparator has that this registry does not: an exterior

The comparative lens mapped the aval, Lloyd's underwriting, academic citation and
retraction, surety, proof-of-stake slashing, and professional attestation. The mappings
fail at a common joint.

- **The aval** works because the endorser is bound by a legal system he did not author.
  Here the identities holding every realized citation pair published the law of endorsement
  itself.
- **Academia** is the closest surface analogue and cuts the other way: its costless
  citation works because the cost is imposed *elsewhere*, by an outside labour market, at a
  scale where no single actor moves the aggregate, on identities that cannot be rotated.
  This registry has `roster.json` empty, `.harness/trust/` absent, and 12 typed edges.
- **Slashing misleads and is the most dangerous item on the list**: it is automatic only
  because the fault is self-evidencing with zero discretion. The proposed trigger here is a
  discretionary written verdict. Attaching an automatic penalty to a discretionary verdict
  is the machine deciding, which clause 4 forbids in its own words.
- Slashing's realized failure was not attack but **centralization** — and this registry is
  already in that state at a price of zero: 4 of 42 pairs, all inside 3 identities.

**The registry's deficit is not a missing mechanism. It is a missing exterior.** The
question that precedes pricing is: what outside this harness would ever consume a standing
figure? Until that has an answer, every pricing scheme is a tariff at a border with no
country on the other side.

The operator's stated goal is an economic and fiscal mechanism. The comparative lens found

- **Live, verified by the coordinator this session:** 54.8% frontier share of raw tokens,
  in-band, exit 0. 6,940 assistant messages deduped by message id.
- **PR-025 clause 1 is already corroborated in the only currency the harness has:**
  verifiers produced 241,507 output tokens against workers' 151,463. Adversarial work
  already costs more. No exchange rate on standing needs to be written to honour it.

None of this consumes a standing figure. PR-022 clause 5 is untouched. PR-026 never comes up.

**Wall-clock is constitutionally dead** — PR-010's ratio rejects it because it "measures
the burstiness of operator sessions, not neglect by the polity", and the data confirms:
median 27 min, p90 1,152 min, max 26.9 days.

**And the incentive asymmetry favours the cost side.** A cost meter's profitable forgery is
under-reporting your own consumption, which yields no standing. A credit meter's profitable
forgery CREATES standing. Deflationary and self-limiting versus inflationary and
compounding. That holds even though neither substrate is secure.

The refutation the comparative lens could not answer, and flagged against itself: **a token
budget disciplines spend and is epistemically silent.** It taxes an agent publishing 18
uncited records exactly as much as it taxes good work, because it cannot tell them apart.
The operator's second clause — how do we stop agents gaming the architecture — is entirely
epistemic, and this answers only the first.

## 8. The three instruments proposed, none requiring a lift

1. **Disclosure, not demotion** (constitutional lens). A per-identity breakout of typed
   citations issued, split by the current status of the cited record. Structurally
   identical to the `by_status` breakout that already ships on `rule_authorship`. Clause 3's
   object is demotion; this is disclosure.
2. **The defeasibility declaration** (mechanism lens). Every `follows`/`reinterprets` must
   name, in the citer's words, the condition of the cited record whose failure would defeat
   the reliance — and it must resolve to a `validity_conditions` or `scope_conditions`
   entry of that record. **You cannot name one without opening the cited record: citing
   requires reading.** Certain, small, uniform, paid at authorship, no adjudication, no
   hazard rate. Feasibility checked: all 59 records carry a non-empty `validity_conditions`,
   and 10 of the 12 typed citations ever issued would have been publishable unchanged.
   Its own drafter's stated defect: a boilerplate declaration collapses the cost to one
   lookup, and the pointer forces the record open but cannot force the reading to be real.
3. **Destruction follows the signature** (attack lens). A seventh figure symmetric to the
   six: `destructive_acts_issued`, counting `overrules`/`voids`/`supersedes`/`distinguishes`
   authored, each shown with its target, the target's author, and the citer count at the
   time. `attest.py`'s design rule is that credit follows the signature so forgery can only
   destroy. Extend it: **destruction should follow the signature too.** It stops nobody, and
   nothing in-process can; it makes the attacker choose a name to be seen under, which is
   the only cost this architecture can impose before PR-023's trust root exists.

**Terms the attack lens would impose, stated as refusals:**
- No consequence may cite an event a single agent can manufacture. By that test
  `overruled` status, `distinguishes`, `task_reopened` and `completed_by` are all
  disqualified as triggers today, all four reproduced as free actions.
- No liability instrument ships before the trust root. A farmed discount misprices; a
  farmed liability TARGETS.
- Fix the free destruction before pricing the free creation.

different and larger proceeding than making citations cost something.
