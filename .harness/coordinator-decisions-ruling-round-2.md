## ANTI-FREEZE DESIGN (F8 addendum, coordinator, 2026-08-15)

### The collision the operator's own rulings create, stated first

F6 (approved) SHRINKS the eligible verifier pool: an author's whole delegation
closure is excluded from verdicting its records. F8 (approved) ADDS a new,
open-ended demand on that same pool: every appeal consumes verifier attention.
Nobody asked for that interaction and it is the sharpest freeze vector we have,
because it does not require a single bad actor - honest disputes alone can starve
the bench. Any anti-freeze design that ignores verification capacity is decorative.

### What the approved F8 design already covers, per record

Deadlines are TTLs with defined expiry actions and silence auto-forwards (H1);
standing is double-filtered (H2); affirm-with-reserve is cheaper to issue than
overrule (H3); deferred effect keeps the record live while repair lands (H4);
review is de novo only on rule violations (H5); the record stays operative during
appeal unless the appellant separately wins a stay, a failed appeal costs the
appellant something recorded, and all grounds must be raised in one petition (H6).
Doc 19 adds that challenge cost decreases with dependents (K1-K3) and that
manufactured dependents are blocked (K6).

### What none of that covers: the aggregate

Every protection above is PER RECORD. A record under appeal stays live. But if a
large share of the registry is under appeal at once, the SYSTEM is frozen even
though no single record is, because nothing can be built on with confidence. Five
additions, all aggregate-level:

AF-1. DOCKET CAP. A registry-wide ceiling on concurrent open appeals, expressed as
a share of active records and as an absolute count. Beyond the cap, petitions queue
rather than open. This is Fukuyama's veto-point budget made mechanical: the number
of actors who can simultaneously suspend anything is bounded by construction, not
by good behaviour.

AF-2. APPEAL CAPACITY IS METERED AGAINST VERIFICATION CAPACITY. Appeals may not
consume more than a declared share of verifier throughput, because verifying new
work is the system's primary function and appeal is secondary. This is the direct
answer to the F6 collision: when the eligible pool shrinks, the appellate docket
shrinks with it automatically, instead of silently crowding out production.

AF-3. THE DELAY COSTS THE DELAYER, SCALED TO THE DELAY. H6 already makes a failed
appeal cost something. Strengthen it: the cost scales with how long the record was
clouded, not merely with the loss. CEQA's structural bug is that the petitioner
bears zero delay cost while the project bears all of it; an appellant who imposes
six months of uncertainty and loses must carry a burden proportional to those six
months.

AF-4. STANDING KEYED TO A LIVE BINDING, NOT TO AN OPINION. Sharpening H2: the
appellant must have a live task that the challenged record actually binds. No
abstract or prospective interest qualifies. This forecloses the professional-
litigant agent, which is the role the economics epic would otherwise make
profitable.

AF-5. THE TRIPWIRE ACQUIRES TEETH. Doc 16's H12 tripwire only reports. Give its
high side an automatic consequence: when appellate volume crosses the threshold,
standing tightens and the docket cap binds harder until volume recedes. Self-
regulating, so a freeze does not wait on an operator who may be asleep.

### AF-6. The verb ships with a sunset, not with permanence

The operator's underlying fear is that we build a permanent vetocracy. The
republican answer is not a promise but an expiry: the appellate verb is enacted for
a bounded term and lapses unless affirmatively renewed on evidence. The renewal
test is doc 16's H11 health metric - the verdict distribution and the catch-rate,
never appeal volume. If strikes dominate while affirm-with-reserve stays rare, the
verb is blocking rather than improving and it dies by default rather than surviving
by inertia. This converts an irreversible institutional commitment into a revocable
experiment, which is the only honest way to ship something whose failure mode is
exactly the disease it treats.

### AF-7. Sequencing, applied to ourselves

Doc 19's whole finding is that armour is temporal: contest before committing. The
same rule binds this epic. The appellate verb ships FIRST, with AF-1 through AF-6,
and runs measured for a full term BEFORE the economics epic attaches material
stakes to standing. Shipping both at once would create the litigation incentive and
the litigation channel in the same act, with no baseline to tell one effect from
the other. The economics epic remains research-only until the appellate baseline
exists.

Calibration note: every threshold above is a number to be set from the measured
baseline the F10 instrumentation now produces, not guessed in advance. AF-1 and
AF-2 in particular should be calibrated against observed verifier throughput, and
the doc 19 tension stands - a record can currently reach top tier on a single
application, so the cheap-contest interval that AF-1's early window presumes does
not exist yet and must be created before the curve can be calibrated.

## F10 EXECUTION, FIRST FINDING (coordinator, 2026-08-15): the terna is not free

The GO on D-8 was acted on immediately by measuring the substrate the terna was
supposed to be derived from. The measurement invalidates the "zero cost, already in
events.jsonl" characterisation the dossier carried. Recorded as a defect, not
worked around.

MEASURED, replayable:
- `.harness/logs/events.jsonl` holds 13,193 events. 10,496 of them - 80 percent -
  fall on the single day 2026-08-11.
- `precedent_published` appears 5,186 times but resolves to only 61 distinct record
  ids. `PR-001` alone carries 3,090 publish events, all on 2026-08-11, issued by
  task ids outside this workspace's range (`T-620` among them, 138 times).
- The live registry holds 59 records. The log therefore over-counts publication by
  roughly two orders of magnitude.

DEFECT REGISTERED: TELEMETRY-PROVENANCE-A. The event log mixes production writes
with a development/test burst that predates the live-log isolation guard now
asserted in `test_precedent_bridge.py`. Any catch-rate, incident count or reopen
rate computed over the raw log is dominated by that burst. Minting the terna as
registry records off this baseline would have consecrated noise as a constitutional
figure - the precise failure the F10 instrumentation exists to prevent, committed
by the instrumentation itself.

CONSEQUENCE FOR D-8: the terna's real precondition is a provenance filter with a
declared cut - production writes distinguished from replay/dev writes - plus a
frozen baseline window, both verified before any record is minted. That is a
short but non-zero build. The seeded-defect pilot and the T-349 sampling are
unaffected and remain as ruled.

MEASURED AND SOUND, by contrast: frontier token share reads 0.4699 raw and 0.5988
output against the P-030 band of 0.53 to 0.65, status below-band on raw. This leg
of the terna needs no repair; `token_share.py` computes it from the transcript
directory, not from the event log.

     mandatory finding at the next section-5A audit, exactly as cost_policy rule 1
     already does for a breach, instead of being resolved by fiat. A contradiction
     becomes an alarm, never a silent override.
