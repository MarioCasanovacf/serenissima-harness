## D4-1 (instrument selection): A AND C BUILD; B IS RESCOPED, NOT DEFERRED

RULING, operator, verbatim: *"de la D1 tenemos que hacer A y C, y dejar la B como
condición necesaria para promover cabildeo y legislación."*

Disposition of each instrument:

- **A — disclosure, not subtraction.** BUILD. Per-identity display of citations issued,
  split by the current status of the cited record, no arithmetic on any standing figure.
  Reads existing fields; attaches no consequence; PR-022 clause 4 untouched.
- **C — destruction carries a signature.** BUILD. A seventh figure, symmetric to the six
  of PR-022 clause 3, counting destructive acts issued per identity with target and the
  target's reliance count at time of act. Stops nobody; attributes everyone.
- **B — the defeasibility declaration.** RESCOPED BY THE OPERATOR, from a condition on
  *every typed citation* to a condition on **proposing or amending a record**.

The rescope is a repair, not a reduction, and it repairs three things at once:

1. **It answers B's own author's defect.** The panel member who proposed B flagged that a
   boilerplate declaration collapses the cost to a single lookup. Priced per citation, that
   is fatal. Priced per legislative act, a single lookup of the record you are proposing to
   change is not a loophole; it is the minimum competence the act requires.
2. **It clears PR-022 clause 6 without an amendment.** Clause 6 forbids a verification or
   confirmation burden that *varies by standing*. A declaration owed by everyone who
   proposes a record varies by ACT, never by standing. It is uniform on its face. This is
   the distinction the archived PR-022 amendment failed to find
   (`.harness/draft-amendment-PR-022.md`, three drafting errors recorded there).
3. **It lands where a burden already sits.** PR-025 clause 3 already requires
   pre-registration for adversarial work. The legislative channel is the one place in this
   harness where a declared burden has precedent and a home.

Sequencing note: B is a condition on the legislative channel, so it cannot be built before
that channel is defined. The channel is D4-4. B therefore rides D4-4 and does not block
A or C.

## D4-3 (the fiscal wedge): OPEN — operator asked what the Swiss mechanism taught us

Operator's question, verbatim: *"De la decisión 3. ¿Qué nos enseñó el mecanismo Suizo?"*

Answered from this project's own research, not from general knowledge: T-393,
`docs/precedent-research/14-swiss-fiscal-federalism.md`, 609 lines, commissioned under
ruling round 2 mandate F4 and ratified as D-2 of round 2.3. That research corpus is
GITIGNORED and will not resolve for a reader of this repository, which is why every finding
below is stated in full here with its source rather than cited by path alone.

**The finding that changes the coordinator's D4-3 recommendation.** Hook H-1 of that
document, from the NFA's resource-potential measurement (FFA 2020): the levy attaches to
CAPACITY, never to realized output, specifically so that changing your rate does not change
what you pay. A wedge on token consumption is a levy on realized work. It is Swiss in the
lowness of its rate and anti-Swiss in its base.

The accurate mapping of the existing token instrument is **H-9, the debt brake**, not the
wedge: a hard budget constraint on ordinary system overhead, with surges allowed only
against a mandatory amortization schedule. PR-049 plus `token_share.py` is a spending
discipline. It is not, and was never, a levy on consolidation.

Four further lessons that bind the F4 formula whenever it is drafted:

- **H-4 / H-5, the absorption ceilings.** Schwyz retains on the order of 95 of 100 marginal
  francs (IWP 01/2024). Above a few percent of marginal absorption, the competition the
  burden exists to police dies. The recipient side is capped too: the Jura 25% warning is
  imported as a constraint, not repeated.
- **H-12, tax the stock, never the realization** — with a confiscation guardrail (BGE 106
  Ia 342, 1980) and growth forbearance (Zurich start-up NAV practice), so paper standing
  cannot crush an illiquid innovator.
- **H-16, repeal passes when paired and fails when naked.** Corporate Tax Reform III was
  rejected 59.1% in 2017; TRAF passed 66.4% in 2019 only once coupled with AHV pension
  funding. Abolishing a grandfathered standing class requires transitional compensation to
  the losing cohort, stated in the abolishing record.
- **H-19, leniency for the first defector.** The first member of a confirmation cartel to
  self-report receives a sanction waiver (KG 2003 Bonusregelung). Convergent with doc 12's
  finding that disclosure alone did not police the Royal Society's Newton-Leibniz
  committee. This is the sharpest instrument in the document for the problem this harness
  actually has.

**The load-bearing counterexample, which the document puts up front and which the
coordinator will not bury.** Switzerland was a legal cartel paradise until 1995
(Heinemann 2021). Jurisdictional competition did not produce competition within markets.
The federation never internally dislodged its own privileged class of holding companies;
that took EU and OECD external pressure plus a compensation-coupled referendum. The
pre-1995 abuse-only cartel regime's binding-order path was used TWICE IN 33 YEARS.

Read onto this harness: an internal mechanism does not dislodge its own incumbents, and an
enforcement posture that fires only after an adjudicated finding does not fire.

**Where this leaves D4-3.** The Swiss template says the levy attaches to consolidated
standing. PR-026 clause 3 puts consequences keyed to computed standing out of reach. So
D4-3 and D4-4 are not two questions. The fiscal wedge the operator wants is precisely the
instrument the collision on D4-4 governs.

OWED, and now called in by this question: the explainer artifact mandated in round 2.3
D-2 — the Swiss system itself, as a system the operator expects to inhabit, plus its F4
mapping and its harness implications.

## D4-4 (the PR-023 / PR-026 collision): OPEN — operator proposed a constituent assembly

Operator's question, verbatim: *"¿Por qué no lo sometemos a un concilio/senado que debata
el tema? Una asamblea constituyente, muy a lo founding fathers."*

**The form is right and the coordinator did not propose it.** The collision between
PR-023 clause 1 (verification-burden discounts scheduled as Phase B's first instrument)
and PR-026 clause 3 at natural width (which forbids them permanently) is not a defect in a
draft. It is two supreme rules that need reconciling. A blind panel is a court: it reviews
a proposal it did not write. Reconciling two constitutional records is a LEGISLATIVE act,
and this harness has no legislature. Every constitutional record it has was published by a
worker.

**The blocking constraint, stated before the design.** D-17: every agent runs as the same
OS user on one filesystem, so no agent can hold a secret from another. Seven delegates
convened today are seven prompts from one process. An assembly whose members cannot be
distinguished from one another reproduces, with more ceremony and at larger scale, exactly
the pathology this docket found: PR-019, PR-022, PR-023, PR-024, PR-025 and PR-026 — six
tier-1 records, one identity, one task, zero confirmations.

Measured state of the identity layer as of this ruling: `roster.py roster` reports zero
colegiados, zero plazas, zero issued names. `.harness/trust/` does not exist.

**What makes it real.** Under the establishment model the operator ratified, a plaza is a
seat signed by the operator's key before any occupant exists. An assembly whose seats live
inside signed warrants has a composition an attacker cannot forge, bounded absolutely by
what was signed. That key is the only boundary in this architecture that is not a string
comparison the attacker controls both sides of.

The assembly is therefore BUILDABLE and GATED, on the five commands in
`.harness/OPERATOR-ENROLMENT.md`. Nothing else in the design is waiting on anything else.

**Procedure available off the shelf**, from the Swiss document rather than invented:

- **H-20, militia not magistracy.** Delegates are working agents sitting part-time, never a
  permanent judging caste, with reputational exposure CAPPED — the Swiss candidate-scarcity
  failure (Contradiction 11) shows uncapped liability prices out honest servers.
- **H-14, concordat quorum.** The assembly's act binds at N ratifications, binds only
  ratifiers, stays permanently open to late joiners, and must state actual coverage rather
  than claimed universality.
- **H-13, referendum threat.** Low-standing identities hold a cheap challenge-trigger
  against the assembly's output, calibrated so it cannot become a universal veto.
- **H-23, one vehicle.** Constitutional-grade reform passes bundled and fails piecemeal.
  The PR-023/PR-026 collision goes to the assembly as one package, not as two motions.

**The Philadelphia problem, named so the design does not walk into it.** The 1787
convention was convened to amend the Articles and instead wrote a new constitution. It
could not cure that from inside; ratification happened in conventions outside itself. The
analog binds here: an assembly cannot vote itself the authority to resolve a tier-1
collision. The operator ratifies its output, or the assembly is a debating society whose
minutes look like law.

RECOMMENDATION: convene it, after enrolment, as the vehicle for D4-3 and D4-4 jointly.
