# Operator rulings, 2026-10-08: damages are paid in operational budget

The operator ruled on decision 2 of the decision sheet cut on 2026-10-07. This file is the
durable record. Nothing was signed, and nothing needed to be: the ruling attaches no
consequence, so no warrant carries it.

## Decision 2: the unit of damage is operational budget

**Question.** When a contract is breached, in what unit is the damage measured and paid?

**Ruling: operational budget.** Operator's words: *"Adelante la idea del presupuesto
operativo como costo de indemnización."*

The breaching party compensates with part of its own budget: tokens, tasks or deadline
days that the contract commits. Reputation is never the unit. PR-022 clause 4, PR-026 and
PR-061 stay intact.

**Why this sits with the operator.** The unit in which a consequence is counted fixes how a
breach is evaluated, and PR-060 clause 5 resolves doubt toward constitutional. PR-060
clause 3 gives constitutional parameters to the operator alone. The coordinator offers this
reading for checking, not for trust; its tier-1 citation record carries four errors.

**What it does NOT do.** It attaches no consequence to anything. PR-060 clause 6 holds:
every individual consequence still needs its own published record, naming the operational
parameter, the consequence, and the agent's own act of setting that parameter. The
coordinator must not treat "the operator said yes" as that record, the same caution the
2026-08-17 ruling D2 recorded.

**Open and not decided here.**

- What a contract is, what counts as breach, whose budget pays and who receives it. These
  are the design T-436 drafts and T-437 attacks, with no code, before the operator reads it.
- Who hears a claim of breach. That is decision 3, still pending.
- Who signed a contract. Identity is still self-asserted (D-17), so a contract binds a name,
  not a proven agent. Decision 1 is still pending.
