# Marketing Surfaces

Use for landing pages, campaigns, product launches, pricing, portfolios, and other surfaces whose
primary job is communication and conversion.

## Shape the conversion story

1. Define one audience, one proposition, one primary action, and the evidence needed to make that
   action credible.
2. Order sections by decision logic: recognition, promise, mechanism, evidence, objection handling,
   and action. Change this sequence when the audience's actual decision requires it.
3. Give the hero enough specificity to identify the product and outcome. Keep the primary action
   visible without forcing every supporting idea above the fold.
4. Use authentic proof only. Preserve legal qualifiers and disclose missing case studies, numbers,
   logos, awards, or screenshots rather than inventing them.
5. Keep repeated calls to action semantically consistent. A secondary action should support a
   different level of commitment, not relabel the same destination.

## Create distinctiveness from evidence

Derive the visual signature from brand assets, product behavior, audience culture, content, or a
specific metaphor. Let one or two devices carry the identity: a type relationship, image treatment,
composition rule, color behavior, or interaction motif. Do not stack every fashionable effect.

Use imagery where it adds product comprehension, emotional context, evidence, or narrative. A page
does not require one image per section. Reserve space and responsive crops for final assets.

## Protect the production surface

- Preserve destination URLs, anchors, analytics events, experiments, consent behavior, forms, SEO
  metadata, canonical and social metadata, structured data, and legal copy unless authorized.
- Validate form labels, errors, success paths, spam or consent affordances, and keyboard behavior.
- Keep core content server-readable or otherwise compatible with the project's SEO strategy.
- Check responsive copy wrapping, navigation collapse, media crops, cumulative layout shift, and
  performance of fonts, video, animation, and above-fold imagery.

Conversion quality means a clear, credible decision path. It does not mean maximizing the number of
buttons, urgency devices, or animated sections.
