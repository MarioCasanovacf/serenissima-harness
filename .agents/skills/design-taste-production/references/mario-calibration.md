# Mario controlled-pair calibration

Use calibration to test profile behavior, not to tune a specimen after seeing its score.

1. Freeze one fixture, claim set, surface brief, viewport, and visible-copy language.
2. Produce baseline A from the prior skill without a Mario profile; produce candidate B from the
   revised skill with `personal:mario`; produce B-no-flag from that same revised skill without the
   flag as the negative control.
3. Record prompt, skill path and hash, profile flag, assumptions, render command, and QA command
   for every variant. Assign blind specimen labels and reveal identity only after scoring.
4. Score structural Mario fidelity, semantic data communication, surface translation, veracity,
   hierarchy, accessibility, and production quality. Reject any unsupported claim, unstable color
   meaning, color-only status, profile leak, clipping, missing artifact, or surface regression.
5. Promote only when every hard gate passes, the candidate clears the declared improvement
   threshold, no surface regresses, and the no-flag output remains brand-neutral. Leave the final
   human gate pending until Mario says he would publish every requested piece as his own.

Change the reusable rules only after the controlled comparison reveals a general failure. Record
the calibration verdict and rationale; do not copy specimen-specific values into the skill.
