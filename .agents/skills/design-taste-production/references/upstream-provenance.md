# Upstream Provenance

This skill is an original stack-neutral adaptation inspired by design judgment, brief inference,
visual-direction dials, anti-generic heuristics, redesign discipline, motion restraint,
accessibility, and preflight concepts from:

- Project: Leonxlnx/taste-skill
- Upstream URL: https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill
- Pinned source commit: b17742737e796305d829b3ad39eda3add0d79060
- Relevant upstream source: skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md and its sibling specialist skills
- License: MIT

The adaptation expands the usable scope to product UI, dashboards, transactional and multi-step
flows, reference-to-code work, visual review, and image concept direction. It preserves the target
project's framework and product contracts, treats motion and image tools as conditional, and does
not inherit mandatory assumptions about React, Next.js, Tailwind, GSAP, dark mode, fonts, remote
tools, punctuation, random aesthetic selection, or a fixed image-per-section rule.

The complete upstream license is retained in `../LICENSE.upstream`. Keep the source URL, pinned
commit, provenance note, and license together when redistributing or revising this adaptation.
