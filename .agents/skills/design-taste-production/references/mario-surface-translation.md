# Mario surface translation

Carry one worldview across surfaces without copying one layout. Preserve the stable type,
material, voice, and semantic-color grammar, then let each surface perform its own job.

- **Dashboard:** reveal state, change, exception, period, units, freshness, and the next action
  within five seconds. Use direct labels and annotations; do not turn the page into a card grid.
- **Personal landing:** establish Mario's authorship, method, and bounded evidence. Present work as
  analytical practice, not as a fictional SaaS product, client engagement, or conversion funnel.
  Limit the hero to one evidence module. Align every displayed number to a common grid or baseline;
  keep units and ISO periods intact, and never rotate an analytical figure.
- **Case study or report:** form a monographic argument from question, data, method, findings,
  limits, and decision. Give figures room, captions, units, and sources; use diagrams as evidence.
- **Executive presentation:** make the conclusion and requested decision primary. Use a sequence
  of claims rather than shrinking a dashboard into slides.

Keep responsive hierarchy and reading order intact. A narrow web view may stack evidence and move
annotations, but must preserve labels, comparisons, limitations, and actions. A presentation may
compress prose, but must not remove the period, units, source, or uncertainty that bounds a claim.
